## 1. The baseline: what an AI-using business needs in place

| # | Policy element | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Approved tools & accounts | Which AI tools are sanctioned, on which accounts/tiers; how to request new ones |
| 2 | Data rules | What data may enter which tools (traffic-light model from the privacy review) |
| 3 | Output verification | Who checks AI output before it reaches decisions, clients, or production; how that's recorded where it matters |
| 4 | Client-facing disclosure | When/whether AI-assisted work is disclosed to clients; who decides |
| 5 | Unattended automation | Approval + human checkpoint requirements for anything acting without per-output review |
| 6 | Employment-context AI | Rules for AI in hiring/evaluation/monitoring (legally sensitive — drafted with counsel) |
| 7 | IP & provenance | Ownership stance on AI-assisted work; keeping prompts/records where provenance matters |
| 8 | Incident reporting | Blame-free path to report a bad output or data slip, and who responds |
| 9 | Training & acknowledgment | Onboarding + periodic refresh; policy sign-off |
| 10 | Ownership & review | Named policy owner; review cadence tied to inventory refresh |
