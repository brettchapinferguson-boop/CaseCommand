# Confidential Employee AI Usage Survey

**Client:** {{Client Company}} · **Fielded:** {{dates}} · **Tool:** {{survey platform}}

> GUIDANCE: The survey only works if people believe it's safe to answer
> honestly. Field it through a neutral channel, keep it under 10 minutes,
> and never report results in a way that could identify an individual
> (suppress any cut with fewer than 5 respondents). Leadership gets
> aggregates only.

## Introduction shown to employees

> {{Client Company}} has asked Anchor AI Solutions, an outside consultant, to
> understand how AI tools are actually being used across the company — so it
> can support what's working and fix what isn't. **This survey is anonymous.**
> Anchor reports only aggregated results; no individual responses are shared
> with your employer. There are no wrong answers, and nothing you report here
> will be used against anyone. Honest answers — including about unofficial
> tools — are the most useful thing you can give us. ~8 minutes.

## Section 4 — Data and review practices

## Section 5 — Rules and support

16. Anything else we should know about how AI is really used here?
    (Free text, optional)
