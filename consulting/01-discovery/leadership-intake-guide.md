# Leadership Intake Interview Guide

**Engagement:** Discovery — {{Client Company}}
**Interviewee:** {{Name, Title}} · **Interviewer:** {{Consultant}} · **Date:** {{Date}}
**Length:** 60–90 minutes

> GUIDANCE: This is a conversation guide, not a script. Get the story in the
> interviewee's own words; the follow-ups in brackets are prompts to use only
> if the topic doesn't surface naturally. Capture verbatim quotes where
> possible — they anchor the snapshot report.

## A. The Business (10 min)

1. Walk me through what the business does and how it makes money.
2. Headcount and shape — which teams are biggest? Which are growing?
3. What are the top 2–3 priorities for the next 12 months?
4. What's the most painful, repetitive work in the company right now?
   [Where does work pile up? What do people complain about?]

## B. Current AI Usage (20 min)

5. What AI tools has the company *officially* adopted or paid for?
   [ChatGPT/Claude/Copilot seats? AI features inside existing SaaS?]
6. What do you *suspect* people are using unofficially?
7. Who is the heaviest AI user in the company? What do they do with it?
8. Any AI agents or automations running unattended? [Zapier/Make flows,
   AI email responders, chatbots, scheduled scripts?]
9. Has anything already gone wrong — a bad output shipped, data pasted
   somewhere it shouldn't be, a client noticing AI-generated work?

## C. Data and Confidentiality (15 min)

10. What's the most sensitive data the company handles?
    [Client/customer data, financials, health data, trade secrets?]
11. Do you know whether that data is being put into AI tools today?
12. Any contractual or regulatory promises about that data?
    [Client confidentiality clauses, HIPAA, privacy policies, DPAs?]

## D. Policy and Governance (10 min)

13. Is there a written AI or acceptable-use policy? When was it last touched?
14. Has anyone been trained on AI use? Is anyone accountable for it?
15. If an employee wanted to adopt a new AI tool tomorrow, what would they
    have to do? [Honest answer — is there any gate at all?]

## E. Ambitions and Concerns (10 min)

16. Where do you *want* AI to take the business in 1–2 years?
17. What's your biggest worry about AI — competitively and internally?
18. If this engagement could answer one question, what would it be?

## F. Logistics (5 min)

19. Who else should we talk to? Who actually knows how work gets done?
20. Best channel to distribute a short anonymous employee survey?
21. Any documents you can share now? [Policies, tool list, org chart.]
